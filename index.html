    Your privacy

    Nothing you enter here is collected or sent anywhere — not to cSPA, not to anyone. There are no accounts, no tracking cookies, and no analytics.

    Your choices and notes are saved only on this device, in this browser, so you can pick up where you left off. Nobody else can see them, and they never leave your device.

    You can erase them at any time with “Clear my saved answers” on your ballot card. They're also erased automatically a month after the election. Printing or copying your card is still the best way to keep a lasting copy.

    This browser is blocking local storage (private browsing can do this), so nothing can be saved on this device. Instead, your choices are kept in this page's web address, which stays on your device — your browser may keep it in your history, like any address.
